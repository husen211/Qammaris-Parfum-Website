## Why Qammaris Exists — first-party source
 A few years ago, I wasn't someone who knew much about fragrances.
For me, buying a perfume was simple: if it smelled good, that was enough.
Everything changed when I moved to Jakarta for university.

There, I met friends who were genuinely passionate about fragrances. Conversations about perfumes were everywhere—different scents for different occasions, different moods, different personalities. That was when I realized that fragrance isn't just about smelling good. It's about confidence, self-expression, and how we present ourselves to the world.

I started exploring the fragrance world myself. Local brands, designer fragrances, niche perfumes, and eventually Middle Eastern fragrances.
During that journey, I discovered something interesting.
Many high-quality fragrances with excellent performance didn't have to cost millions of rupiah. Some offered incredible value while still delivering unique and memorable scent profiles.

The real turning point happened when I returned to Palu.
I wanted to buy another fragrance, but there was one problem: I couldn't try it first. In Jakarta, I could visit stores, test fragrances, compare options, and decide what suited me best. Back home, most purchases meant ordering online and hoping I would like the scent when it arrived.

The issue is that fragrance is highly personal. A review can say a perfume smells amazing. A friend can highly recommend it. But everyone's nose is different. That's when a simple question stayed in my mind: "Why should people have to buy fragrances without experiencing them first?" That question eventually became the foundation of Qammaris.

Qammaris was built with a simple vision: to create a place where people can explore, test, compare, and discover fragrances that truly match their preferences before making a purchase. Not because a fragrance is trending.
Not because someone on the internet said it's good. But because it genuinely feels right for them. 

The journey hasn't been easy. Renovating the store, securing official distribution channels, and building everything from the ground up has taken far more time and effort than I initially expected.
But every step has brought us closer to making that vision a reality.
This is only the beginning, and we're excited for what's ahead.
— Husein



## The Story Behind Qammaris — first-party source

What started as a personal frustration eventually became a business idea.
Finding and exploring fragrances wasn't always easy. Many products could only be purchased online, and customers often had to buy without ever experiencing the scent firsthand.

That experience led to the idea of Qammaris.
Over the last few months, we've been transforming an unused space into a fragrance retail store, navigating distributor approvals, designing the customer experience, and building the foundation for something we believe will create value for our community.

Qammaris wasn't built overnight. It was built through months of planning, learning, problem-solving, and persistence.
We're looking forward to sharing more of the journey.
